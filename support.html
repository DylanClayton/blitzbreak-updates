BLITZBREAK · Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us and a person will answer.

Contact

Email support@playblitzbreak.com. Say which device you play on and your game version (shown on the main menu), and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Can't play online

  • "Update required": online games only match players on the same version. Update from the store you got Blitzbreak from.
  • Can't connect to the servers: check your internet connection and try again in a few minutes. If it keeps happening, email us.
  • Your friend can't join your party: everyone in a party needs the same version, and a party holds up to four players.

You can always play offline against bots, or host a private game on a computer.

Controllers

Xbox, PlayStation and most other controllers work for play and in the menus: A (or Cross) picks, B (or Circle) goes back. Typing, such as a friend code, still needs a keyboard.

Refunds and purchases

Blitzbreak is bought through a store (Steam, the Epic Games Store, the App Store or Google Play), and that store handles refunds under its own policy. Ask them first; if they can't help, email us.

Your account and data

To delete your online account, open Settings > Delete online account in the game. What we keep and how to delete it without the game: see the privacy policy.
