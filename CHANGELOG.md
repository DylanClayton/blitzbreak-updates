# Blitzbreak changelog

What changed in each release, for players. Versions are X.Y.Z: players can play together when X
and Y match, and Z releases are fixes and polish that don't affect who you can play with.

## 0.4.0

- Customize your brawler: pick a body (Standard, Big or Huge), helmet, eyes, G-Hammer and E-Sword, and drag the preview to turn it. Your look now shows in online games, and looks added in later updates show up as the closest default for anyone who hasn't updated yet. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Rejoin your seat: if you drop out of an online match, a bot keeps your seat warm for 60 seconds. Reconnect with the Rejoin button and you're back in, with your score. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Host controls in the lobby: the host can kick players or hand hosting to someone else, and everyone can mark themselves Ready. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- More match settings: a round time limit (when it runs out, nobody scores and the next round starts), the respawn time, and hammers-only or swords-only matches. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- In first person you now see your brawler's arm holding your weapon, and both hands around the bomb, with a fuller overhead swing. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Menus, the lobby and the pause menu stay clear of the camera cut-out on phones. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Fixes for error messages when quitting the game. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.3.3

- New look: everyone is now a Toy Brawler, a chunky robot with a helmet, glowing eyes behind the visor and your team's colors, animated as it runs, jumps, swings and carries the bomb. Bots show off the different helmets, eye styles and body types. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- New weapons: three G-Hammers (Maul, Gravity Core, Warhammer) and three E-Swords (Blade, Curved, Cleaver), glowing in your accent color. They only change how weapons look, not how they hit. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.3.2

- Music: a track for the menu and lobby, another for matches, and a short fanfare when a match is won or lost. Set its volume with the new Music slider in Settings. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Combat sounds: a tick when your hit lands and a ping for a kill, a crack when your shield breaks and a hum as it recharges, a heartbeat when you're in danger, plus jump and respawn sounds. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- A new announcer voice. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The announcer is easier to hear over the action, the game goes muffled behind the pause menu, and on computers it mutes while in the background (you can turn that off in Settings). — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- "More details" in the update notice opens the full changelog in your browser. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.3.1

- The main menu title fits on narrow phone screens instead of hiding behind the Play panel. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Watch Bots is gone from the menu: to watch the bots, choose Spectate in the Play vs Bots lobby. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The lobby keeps one size as people join, switch teams or spectate; the teams and settings scroll, and Leave and Start Match stay at the bottom. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.3.0

- Spectators in hosted games: lobbies hold up to 16 people, and once both teams are full, new arrivals spectate. Spectate from the lobby, or switch mid-match from the menu. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Take an open seat mid-match: spectators can join any seat a bot is playing, including seats left by people who disconnected. Joining a match in progress lets you pick a team or keep watching. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Lowering the team size in the lobby moves the extra players to spectating instead of refusing. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Spectating on phones: touch controls for camera modes, targets and first person. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The main menu tells you when an update is out, and joining a game on a different version says who needs to update. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Fixed: errors when the window was resized after playing a match. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.2.0

- iPhone and iPad (via TestFlight) and Android, with touch controls. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Better controller support: finer aiming, and on-screen prompts match what you're using. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The Mac version is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens without a security warning. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.1.0

- First playtest build: online play (Host Game / Join Game) with a lobby, bots in empty seats, four courts, and Windows, Mac and Linux builds. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
