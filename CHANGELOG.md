# Blitzbreak changelog

What changed in each release, for players. Versions are X.Y.Z: players can play together when X
and Y match, and Z releases are fixes and polish that don't affect who you can play with.

## 0.3.0

- Spectators in hosted games: lobbies hold up to 16 people, and once both teams are full, new arrivals spectate. Spectate from the lobby, or switch mid-match from the menu. — @DylanClayton
- Take an open seat mid-match: spectators can join any seat a bot is playing, including seats left by people who disconnected. Joining a match in progress lets you pick a team or keep watching. — @DylanClayton
- Lowering the team size in the lobby moves the extra players to spectating instead of refusing. — @DylanClayton
- Spectating on phones: touch controls for camera modes, targets and first person. — @DylanClayton
- The main menu tells you when an update is out, and joining a game on a different version says who needs to update. — @DylanClayton
- Fixed: errors when the window was resized after playing a match. — @DylanClayton

## 0.2.0

- iPhone and iPad (via TestFlight) and Android, with touch controls. — @DylanClayton
- Better controller support: finer aiming, and on-screen prompts match what you're using. — @DylanClayton
- The Mac version is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens without a security warning. — @DylanClayton

## 0.1.0

- First playtest build: online play (Host Game / Join Game) with a lobby, bots in empty seats, four courts, and Windows, Mac and Linux builds. — @DylanClayton
