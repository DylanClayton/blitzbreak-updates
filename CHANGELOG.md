# Blitzbreak changelog

What changed in each release, for players. Versions are X.Y.Z: players can play together when X
and Y match, and Z releases are fixes and polish that don't affect who you can play with.

## 0.3.0

- Spectators in hosted games: lobbies hold up to 16 people, and once both teams are full, new
  arrivals spectate. Spectate from the lobby, or switch mid-match from the menu.
- Take an open seat mid-match: spectators can join any seat a bot is playing, including seats left
  by people who disconnected. Joining a match in progress lets you pick a team or keep watching.
- Lowering the team size in the lobby moves the extra players to spectating instead of refusing.
- Spectating on phones: touch controls for camera modes, targets and first person.
- The main menu tells you when an update is out, and joining a game on a different version says
  who needs to update.
- Fixed: errors when the window was resized after playing a match.

## 0.2.0

- iPhone and iPad (via TestFlight) and Android, with touch controls.
- Better controller support: finer aiming, and on-screen prompts match what you're using.
- The Mac version is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens without a security warning.

## 0.1.0

- First playtest build: online play (Host Game / Join Game) with a lobby, bots in empty seats,
  four courts, and Windows, Mac and Linux builds.
