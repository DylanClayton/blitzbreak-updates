# Blitzbreak changelog

What changed in each release, for players. Versions are X.Y.Z: players can play together when X
and Y match, and Z releases are fixes and polish that don't affect who you can play with.

## 0.9.4

- A beam of light rises from the bomb in the color of the team carrying it (orange while it's loose), so even in a pile of players you can tell who has it. Thanks to play-tester Kirsten for the idea! — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- If your party's leader leaves while searching for a game, everyone else is told the search stopped, and who's leading now. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.9.3

- Fixed a crash when leaving the main menu (for example, starting a game vs Bots) while a Black Hole, Lightning Storm or Ripples effect was playing in the background. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.9.2

- If someone leaves your party while you're searching for a game, the search stops and says who left, instead of carrying on without them. Search again when you're ready. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.9.1

- Eight choices for every part of your look: new bodies (Reactor, Sculpted, Tin Can, Jetpack, Circuit and more), helmets (Centurion, Halo, Top Hat...), a Cyclops eye, five new hammers and swords, and new hammer slams and bomb blasts, from Crystal Spikes to a Black Hole. "Big" and "Huge" bodies are now Reactor and Sculpted. Players still on 0.9.0 see the default in place of the new ones. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The Play menu puts online first: Casual is the big button, and vs Bots moves to the bottom under Offline. In a party, bot matches and private games wait until you leave it. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The Mac App Store and TestFlight version now signs in to Game Center, so online play works on it. When you're not signed in, the menu says where to do it. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.9.0

- Online play (casual matches, parties and friends) now needs you signed in to the store you got Blitzbreak from: Steam, Epic Games, Game Center or Google Play Games. Matches against bots and private games you host or join still work without one, and the menu says what to do when you're not signed in. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Everyone's store icon shows by their name: on name tags, the scoreboard, the lobby, your party and your friends list. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Getting ready for Steam and the Epic Games Store: those versions sign you in with your Steam or Epic account, and your name there is your name in the game. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- A party searching for a game keeps searching when someone joins or leaves it. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.9

- No more freeze the first time a hammer slam, bomb blast or other effect shows up: they're readied when the game starts. Most noticeable on phones and right after an update. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Controllers work in the menus: A presses the highlighted button (including Play again and Menu after a match) and B goes back. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- In a party, you can't un-ready once your leader is searching for a game. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- On the Mac App Store and TestFlight version, your name is your Game Center name, as on iPhone and iPad. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.8

- Friends: everyone has a friend code to share. Add friends by theirs, see who's on the menu, in a party or in a match, and invite them to your party with one tap; they get a pop-up saying who it's from, and can join for five minutes. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The main menu has a card beside its buttons with tabs for your party, your friends and friend requests. The party moved there from the Play page. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Your name comes with you: on iPhone and iPad it's your Game Center name, and on Android, signing in with Google Play Games gives you your gamer name and keeps your online account when you switch phones. Customize shows where it comes from. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Knocked-out robots no longer get in anyone's way: a sword lunge that finishes someone carries on instead of stopping dead against them, and they disappear after three seconds. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.7

- On phones and tablets, menus no longer outline a button as if it were already chosen when a screen opens. Keyboards and controllers still get the outline. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- "▼ MORE" at the bottom of a long list scrolls it when tapped, and now also shows on the Play and Settings pages. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Settings that are on turn green, so orange only marks the main thing to press. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- iPhone and iPad open on the Blitzbreak logo instead of Godot's. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Mac: hosting a game explains why it asks to use your local network. Testers with a Mac can also install Blitzbreak from TestFlight. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.6

- Casual search waits 30 seconds for other players before starting a game with bots, down from a minute. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Phones draw sharper shadows: no more jagged, crawling edges along the walls. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.5

- Parties ready up: each member presses Ready on the Play page, and the leader can search once everyone's ready. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- At the end of a match the party's leader decides for everyone: Play Again keeps the whole party playing, Main Menu brings everyone back. Members see who they're following, and can leave the party to choose for themselves. The leader also sees when someone in the party is waiting in the menu. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- If you join a party while it's playing, Join Sam's game puts you in the same game, on its team, when there's a seat. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Parties survive the online service restarting: the game brings the party back together under the same code. Party codes no longer change a moment after a party is made. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Join Party has a Paste button, for phones where the paste menu doesn't show. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- While a party searches, everyone in it counts as searching. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- When the game can't reach the Blitzbreak servers, it says what failed, to pass on if you report it. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.4

- From Google Play, the "new version is out" notice on the main menu has an Update button that opens Blitzbreak in the Play Store. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- That notice no longer runs under the main menu's buttons on phones. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.3

- A new app icon: the robot with the bomb, on every platform. On Android it fits the phone's icon shape, and themed icons (Android 13 and later) show the robot instead of Godot's logo. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.2

- Parties: on the Play page, Create Party gives you a code to share, and friends type it into Join Party (up to four of you, on any platform). The leader presses Casual and the whole party finds a game together, on one team: a running game with room for all of you, else other players, else bots after a minute. Play Again keeps the party together, and the party lasts between matches. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Competitive is gone from the Play page for now: it comes back once there are enough players to fill its games. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.1

- The arrows on the menus' pickers, the Back button, the touch controls' menu button and the lobby's host and ready marks now show on iPhone and iPad too. They were missing there in every version so far. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.8.0

- Play Again: after a casual online match, press Play Again on the final scoreboard to go straight into another match on a new court with everyone else who pressed it. Nobody has to search again, and the bots fill in the empty seats as usual. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Name tags and the BOMB marker are in the comic lettering and stay the same size at any distance, so they're readable across the court and no longer cover your screen up close. A robot right in front of you hides its name. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Online matches are encrypted, and the game checks it's talking to the real match server. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Deleting your online account (Settings > Delete online account) also removes it from past match records. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.7.2

- A new Blitzbreak logo on the title screen. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Play vs Bots: the teams are on the left and the match settings on the right, each scrolling on its own, and a list fades out with "MORE" at the bottom when there's more to see. Customize shows it too. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Customize: your robot stands on a floor against a lighter sky, so Cracks & Rubble and the Ink Bomb are easy to see. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The team scores at the top no longer go blank after joining a match that's already going. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Casual search: no more bots countdown, which jumped around. The clock counts up, and if nobody's around a game with bots still starts within a minute. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- iPhone and iPad: game sound plays with the silent switch on (the volume buttons still work), and music from other apps keeps playing. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The FPS counter stays clear of a phone's rounded corners and notch. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The kill feed no longer shows more than five lines when several knockouts happen at once. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Blitzbreak's privacy policy is at playblitzbreak.com/privacy.html. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.7.1

- Comic Court: courts, robots and weapons are drawn like a comic book, with flat colors and ink outlines. Your team's color looks the same on every court, and night courts glow instead of going dark. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- New effects to match: hammer slams, sword clashes and bomb blasts are bold comic-style bursts instead of glowing domes. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Pick your Hammer slam and Bomb blast in Customize. Slams: Shock Wall, Ground Star, Cracks & Rubble, Sound Effect or Speed Lines. Blasts: Classic, Mushroom Cloud, KA-BOOM!, Confetti, Fireworks or Ink Bomb, in the scoring team's color. Picking one shows it off. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- A new main menu: the menu sits in the middle, and each page opens in its place with a Back button. Customize shows your robot on one side and your picks on the other. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- A new HUD and menus: dark comic panels with ink lines, team score plates at the top, the kill feed in caption boxes, and a crosshair you can see on any court. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Fallen robots get thrown again by hammer hits and bomb blasts, and stop rolling once they land. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- In first person, you hold the bomb in both hands in front of you. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Ramps no longer show stripes and dark patches of shadow. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- New settings: Max frame rate (60 to start with) and Anti-aliasing. Menus run at 60 frames a second at most and the game slows right down while it's in the background, so computers run cooler and quieter. Show FPS shows your frame rate. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Phones: the game runs faster, with a 3D resolution setting to trade sharpness for speed, and settings lists scroll, with sliders that work, in the menu and during a match. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Fixed a switched-on setting sometimes losing its orange highlight. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- What's new opens playblitzbreak.com. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.7.0

- Play Online. Casual drops you into a game with people in it, with bots in the empty seats, and anyone can take a bot's seat at any time; if there's no game, it waits up to a minute for others, then starts one with bots. Competitive searches for a full 4v4 match. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Left an online match that's still going? Rejoin match on the Play page takes you back to your seat. A competitive match can't be swapped for another game: rejoin it, or wait until it's over. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- iPhone and iPad sign in with Game Center, so your online account comes with you to a new device. Settings > Delete online account removes it. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- When playing online needs a newer version of Blitzbreak, the game says so. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Bots have names, like "Sprocket (Bot)". — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- E-Sword: the crosshair turns into a red target when a swing would lunge. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- After an online match, the final scoreboard counts down 25 seconds before heading back by itself. When the player hosting from their computer leaves, everyone is told the host ended the game. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Hosting shows how to let Blitzbreak through your computer's firewall. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Fixed players being frozen in place at the start of the next match, and the game freezing when you leave a lobby you're hosting. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.6.2

- New eyes for your robot: Hearts. Find them in Customize > Eyes. Players still on 0.6.1 see your usual eyes until they update. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.6.1

- Course builder (on computers): Main menu > Course Builder. Lay out your own court on a top-down plan: drag the walls, goals and spawn zones, place blocks, ramps, pads, walkways, platforms and spike pits, and tune their numbers. Mirror keeps both ends fair, pieces snap to the grid and can't be placed anywhere that would break a match, and a 3D preview shows it in any theme. Test it against bots in one click, then save it. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Your saved courts appear in Play vs Bots and Host Game, marked "(yours)"; players who join your game get the court automatically. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- The Playground's sliding platform no longer passes over the bomb's starting spot. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.6.0

- New court pieces: ramps up to raised decks, jump pads (green ones throw you forward, cyan ones straight up and higher still if you land on them from a jump), moving walkways, floating platforms and elevators that carry you (and crush you against the floor or a wall), and spike pits. Knock someone into a pit and the kill is yours. The bomb rides, bounces and resets too. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- A new court, Playground, with every new piece to try. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Jump pad guides: show where forward pads throw you, in Settings > Video (off by default). — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Servers now send their court to everyone who joins, so new courts no longer need an update to play, and hosts will be able to share courts they make. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.5.0

- Court themes: every court now comes in eight looks: Pro Arena, Neon Night, Rooftop Sunset, Junkyard, Frozen Rink, Woodland Road, Outer Space and Hell. The host picks one in the lobby, or Random for a different look every match. Themes only change the scenery, never how a court plays. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.4.2

- A new announcer voice: deeper and punchier, cutting through the action. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.4.1

- Online, red players start each round facing down the court instead of at their own wall, and everyone faces the right way after respawning. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- In first person, weapons face forward: the Warhammer strikes with its big face, and the Curved E-Sword's tip curves the right way. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Lobby settings keep their arrows in place: long names scroll inside the row instead of stretching it, and the settings list keeps its scroll position as things change. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.4.0

- Customize your brawler: pick a body (Standard, Big or Huge), helmet, eyes, G-Hammer and E-Sword, and drag the preview to turn it. Your look now shows in online games, and looks added in later updates show up as the closest default for anyone who hasn't updated yet. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Rejoin your seat: if you drop out of an online match, a bot keeps your seat warm for 60 seconds. Reconnect with the Rejoin button and you're back in, with your score. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Host controls in the lobby: the host can kick players or hand hosting to someone else, and everyone can mark themselves Ready. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- More match settings: a round time limit (when it runs out, nobody scores and the next round starts), the respawn time, and hammers-only or swords-only matches. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- In first person you now see your brawler's arm holding your weapon, and both hands around the bomb, with a fuller overhead swing. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Menus, the lobby and the pause menu stay clear of the camera cut-out on phones. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- Fixes for error messages when quitting the game. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

## 0.3.3

- New look: everyone is now a Toy Brawler, a chunky robot with a helmet, glowing eyes behind the visor and your team's colors, animated as it runs, jumps, swings and carries the bomb. Bots show off the different helmets, eye styles and body types. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
- New weapons: three G-Hammers (Maul, Gravity Core, Warhammer) and three E-Swords (Blade, Curved, Cleaver), glowing in your accent color. They only change how weapons look, not how they hit. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)

- A new announcer voice. — [@DylanClayton](https://github.com/DylanClayton)
